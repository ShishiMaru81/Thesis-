The hardened test set mixes seed rows with six adversarial categories (\cref{tab:hardening}). \Cref{tab:per-category} splits the headline scores of the human-study arms, plus Llama zero-shot, by those categories. Missing-parameter, misspelled-crop and unit-ambiguity rows that are labelled clarify are the probe for invented values; seed out-of-domain rows are the probe for abstention. Multi-crop numeric rows are scored only when every gold depth is within 0.1\,mm; no arm in this table meets that bar.
