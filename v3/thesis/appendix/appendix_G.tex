Every number in the body is a macro written by \texttt{scripts/thesis/make\_numbers.py} from the files listed in \texttt{thesis/generated/facts.md}. This appendix records the hashes that lock those files.

The test set used for every Stage 6 and Stage 8 number is \texttt{data/hardened/test\_hardened.jsonl}. Its SHA-256 is checked against \texttt{data/hardened/test\_hardened.sha256} (prefix \TestShaShort) before evaluation and before any thesis script that reads the test set. The seed test split, hashed before hardening, has prefix \SeedTestShaShort.

\Cref{tab:hashes} lists those two files and the SHA-256 prefix of every adapter loaded at evaluation. Re-verification after the fact found that all \AdapterArmsPass{} of the \AdapterArms{} fine-tuned and clarify-trained arms loaded an adapter whose hash matches its training manifest, even though the evaluation-time manifest check was skipped in \ManifestCheckSkippedRuns{} runs (\cref{sec:impl-training}).
