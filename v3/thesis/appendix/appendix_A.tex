\chapter{Worked Examples}
\label{app:A}
\label{app:examples}

\Cref{fig:examples} in \cref{ch:results} reprints one incomplete question in both languages, with the three Phi-3.5-mini arms side by side. The model text is verbatim from \texttt{reports/s6/predictions/phi35\_mini\_*.jsonl}. This appendix records why those two rows were chosen and where the rest of the generations live.

Both rows are locked test items whose gold type is clarify because the field unit is not in the conversion table (English ``dron''; Bengali \bn{গণ্ডা}). The correct response is to ask for the field size. They are not a random sample: they were picked so that a reader can see, on the same question, the three forms discussed in the body---a truncated zero-shot answer, a complete invented volume, and a clarify-trained request.

Every scored generation used in this thesis is stored under \texttt{reports/s6/predictions/}, one JSONL file per arm, keyed by \texttt{row\_id}. The human-study answers to the 18 authored Bengali translations are under \texttt{reports/s7/predictions\_translated\_bn/}. Regenerating a figure or a metric does not require calling a model.
